Piano Snakes & Ladders

A 25-square race for up to 15 players. Land on a ladder and answer correctly to climb — land on a snake and answer correctly to avoid the slide. Reach square 25 and one last question decides the winner.

Board preview

  • Ladder — answer the piano question right, climb straight up. Miss it, and you simply stay put.
  • Snake — answer right and you're safe on your square. Miss it, and you slide down to the tail.
  • Roll the die and move that many squares. Overshoot the last square? You land exactly on 25 — no exact-count rule, keeps things quick.
  • Square 25 — one final question decides it. Answer right and you win. Miss it and you go back to the square you came from, ready to try again next turn.
  • Great for turn-taking in class: one shared screen, students roll in order.
